Set up a status label

Label a status in three steps

  1. 01

    Open the picker

    Visit the dashboard or map, open the extension, and choose “Pick a status color.”

  2. 02

    Choose a label and pattern

    Click a recurring status, give it a plain-language label, and choose a distinct pattern.

  3. 03

    Read labeled matching statuses

    Matching statuses get badges that do not block clicks. Your rules return on that site.

How labels stay readable and local

▧

Use more than color

Every trained status gets text and a distinct graphic pattern. The legend stays useful in grayscale.

⌂

Keep rules on your device

No account or analytics. Rules stay in local extension storage.

↗

Does not change page controls

Badges cannot receive clicks. Forms, links, and the data behind your page are never changed.

COLOR-MATCHING LIMITS

Limits of color matching

A saved label follows nearby solid colors. Gradients and larger color changes are not matched. Check each label after a site changes. Confirm important status in the source system.

It does not certify accessibility, inspect hidden business data, or modify server records.
