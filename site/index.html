Make your own legend

Three clicks to a clearer dashboard

  1. 01

    Open the picker

    Visit the dashboard or map, open the extension, and choose “Pick a status color.”

  2. 02

    Name the signal

    Click a recurring status, give it a plain-language label, and choose a distinct pattern.

  3. 03

    Use the live legend

    Matching elements get pointer-safe badges. Your rules automatically return on that site.

Built like a personal field tool

▧

More than hue

Every trained state gets text and a graphic pattern, so the legend stays useful in grayscale.

⌂

Stays on your device

No sign-in, server, analytics, or browsing-history collection. Export a JSON backup if you want one.

↗

Leaves controls alone

Badges cannot receive clicks. Forms, links, and the data behind your page are never changed.

CHECK THE TAPE

A helper, not a guarantee

The extension matches rendered pixel colors. Themes, gradients, images, and site redesigns can cause missed or incorrect labels. Confirm critical status in the source system, and retrain a rule whenever a site changes.

It does not certify accessibility, inspect hidden business data, or modify server records.
