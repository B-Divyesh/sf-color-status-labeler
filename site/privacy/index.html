Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective August 28, 2026. Color Status Labeler is designed not to collect your data.

What stays in your browser

The extension stores your site origin, labels, sampled colors, patterns, and on/off setting in local extension storage. This data stays on your device unless you choose “Export backup.” That action creates a file for you to save.

What we collect

Nothing through the extension. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, remote API, or telemetry. The public website does not set cookies or run analytics.

Page access

The extension reads visible page styles so it can find colors you taught it. It does not read passwords, submit forms, change server data, or send page contents anywhere.

Your control

Delete one rule or clear a site from the popup. Removing the extension clears its local storage according to your browser’s behavior. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
